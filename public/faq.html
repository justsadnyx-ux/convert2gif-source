FAQ

Answers to the most common questions.

How do I convert an image to a GIF in Discord?

Send .gif with the image attached, or reply to any message that has an image with .gif. You can also use the slash command /gif and hit Attach a file.

What formats are supported?

The bot supports PNG, JPG, and GIF. The web converter is currently in maintenance.

Why don't images come out animated?

By design. Images are converted into a clean static GIF file — the point is the file format, not motion.

Why is there a rate limit?

Convert2GIF runs on a free infrastructure budget. Limits keep costs down while the service stays free: 3 bot GIFs per 10 minutes, and 10 web conversions per 10 minutes per IP.

Does the bot ever store my images?

No. Images are fetched, converted, and sent immediately. Nothing is kept after conversion. See the Privacy Policy.

Does the web converter upload anything?

No. The web converter runs entirely in your browser — your files never leave your device.

Can I see an image's details first?

Yes — .imginfo (or /imginfo) reports the type, dimensions, size, and whether a GIF is animated or static.
