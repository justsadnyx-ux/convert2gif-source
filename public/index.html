FAQ

Common questions

Where do my files go?

The web converter runs entirely in your browser â€” images and videos are processed locally and never uploaded. The Discord bot processes attachments only long enough to reply with the GIF, then discards them.

Do I need to pay or sign up?

No. Both the web converter and the Discord bot are free. There's no account, no watermark, and no conversion limits beyond a short anti-abuse rate limit.

What formats are supported?

Images: PNG, JPG, WEBP. Video: MP4, MOV, AVI. Existing GIFs are also accepted and re-encoded.

Is there a file size limit?

The web converter accepts files up to 25 MB. For the Discord bot, use an attachment that Discord allows (usually 10 MB for free users, 25 MB with boosted servers).
